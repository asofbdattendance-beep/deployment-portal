# Deployment Portal — Context

## Roles & Permissions

| Role | Scope | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| `centre_user` | Their centre subtree (own + SC_SPs) | On the single combined "Consent & Deploy" page: mark consent (Yes/No), days (1–5), stay-at-bhati, chair pass, and the deployment department — all together, auto-saved (+ Save Draft button) |
| `centre_admin` | Their centre subtree | Same as centre_user (editing power is identical in this design) |
| `aso` | All centres | **Read-only everywhere** (v20): sees every tab + Excel exports, but zero writes — no finalize edits, no master-switch toggles, no lock unlocks, no VSS create/assign |
| `vss_operator` | All centres | Undeployed requested-department deploys + VSS create/assign past lock/deadline/switch (since v37 honors the effective VSS switch for VSS-population rows, fail-closed; regular rows keep the bypass); may deploy consent-No VSS (days auto-set via `daysForDept`); never final (`deployed_department_id`), Finalize, Control Panel, Schedule, overrides, users, or audit |
| `super_admin` | All centres | Creates schedules (with deadline), manages departments (+restriction rules), allocates departments to parent centres, marks schedules done, finalizes deployments; sole writer everywhere + the **Control Panel** |

## Days

No dates. Every schedule spans the fixed 5 days: **WED, THU, FRI, SAT, SUN**. Consent records a sewadar's available **number of days** (1–5), not specific day ticks.

## Flow (redesigned — no phases, no locks, no go-ahead)

1. **Super Admin** creates a schedule (a named visit, e.g. "October 2026 Visit"). A `deadline` (date + time) may be set per schedule.
2. **Super Admin** manages departments and sets **restriction rules** per department:
   - `min_days` — minimum number of consent days (1–5) a sewadar must be available on
   - `requires_stay_at_bhati` — department needs stay-at-bhati sewadars
   - `requires_initiated` — department needs initiated sewadars (from `sewadars.is_initiated`)
3. **Super Admin** allocates departments to **CENTREs** (centres with empty `parent_centre`): pick a department, enter a `max_count` per CENTRE, save all at once. CENTRE + SC_SPs share that quota. (UI terminology: parent centre → **CENTRE**, child centre → **SC_SPs**.)
   - **Centres can lock deployment** once final: every allocated department with deployed sewadars must first have an **incharge** (set via the quota-card picker), then the centre clicks **Lock Deployment** — consent/deployment/incharges (regular + VSS) freeze for that CENTRE until an aso/super_admin unlocks it (Consent Dashboard strip). DB-enforced (v13).
4. **Consent + deployment happen together** on one page (`centre_user`/`centre_admin`): each centre manages sewadars for its **whole subtree** (own centre + SC_SPs), per sewadar setting:
   - Consent for deployment (Yes/No)
   - No. of days available (1–5)
   - Stay at bhati (Yes/No)
   - Chair pass (Yes/No)
   - **Deployment department** (from the superadmin-allocated departments only)
   Everything auto-saves (debounced ~800ms), plus an explicit **Save Draft** button on the VSS and Finalize Deployment tabs that flushes pending edits immediately. Filter + search + sort available. Bulk multi-select actions with confirmation: set consent, days, stay-at-bhati, chair pass, and assign-to-department (per-row eligibility/quota checks, skips listed with reasons).
5. **The deadline governs everything** — after the deadline (or when status = `done`), all editing is disabled in the UI **and** enforced by DB triggers. No centre locks, no superadmin go-ahead. **Exception:** ASO / super_admin may keep editing (final deployed department + optional consent fixes) after the deadline — they are exempted inside `block_after_deadline` / `check_deployment`.
6. **ASO / super_admin finalize deployment** on the extra **Finalize Deployment** tab: they see every sewadar's consent + requested department and set the **deployed (final) department** — all in a **single flat table** (centre shown per row, filterable) rather than centre-wise collapsible sections. Consent inputs are shown read-only and only become editable when *Enable editing* is ticked (explicit, never automatic). **Since v20 only super_admin can tick it — aso accounts see a View-only pill.**
7. **The Control Panel (v21, super_admin-only tab)** grants surgical exceptions on top of the closed portal: open deployment for ALL centres or one CENTRE (subtree included), unlock a single department for a single CENTRE, force VSS creation/deployment open or closed per centre (tri-state: Auto/Open/Closed), and allocate additional departments (all centres or one) with an optional "also open it". An override beats the centre lock, the master switches and the deadline — **never** quotas, restriction rules, a `done` schedule, or ASO-finalized sewadars (those stay frozen via v15/v16). Under an opened override, sewadars whose consent is No may also be deployed (consent-given check relaxes), as long as they hold no final department.

## Rules enforced

- One department per sewadar (requested_dept).
- Sewadar must be eligible: consent days >= dept `min_days`, satisfies `requires_stay_at_bhati`, `requires_initiated`.
- Total assigned across the parent subtree must not exceed `max_count` (enforced in UI quota bars + DB trigger).
- Elderly sewadars (`sewadars.badge_status = 'ELDERLY'`) are excluded from the UI lists and blocked by the DB trigger.
- A sewadar with no prev-year row shows "Were not deployed in last session". Low prev attendance (0,1 always; 2 except TRAFFIC OUTSIDE BHATI) is highlighted; TRAFFIC OUTSIDE BHATI attendance is out of 3, all others out of 5.
- **Available days are NOT user-editable.** Every sewadar (regular + VSS) gets **5 days automatically** for every department, except **OE ESCORTS** which is **fixed at 3 days** — the days column is a read-only pill everywhere (Consent & Deploy, VSS, Finalize Deployment), the value is auto-set whenever the department changes (`daysForDept` in `src/lib/logic.js`), re-applied on load (normalizing legacy rows) and forced at save time, and dropdown/bulk-assign eligibility is judged against the days a row WOULD have after switching — so a row on OE ESCORTS (3 days) can always move to a 5-day department. **DB-enforced too** (`sql/v10_oe_escorts_fixed_days.sql`): OE ESCORTS departments store `min_days = vss_min_days = 3` (enforced on insert/rename by `trg_guard_oe_escorts_min_days`), existing consents for OE ESCORTS rows are backfilled to 3, and a BEFORE trigger (`trg_a_clamp_oe_escorts_days`) forces the consent row to 3 days whenever a deployment is written with an OE ESCORTS requested/final department — so the DB trigger `check_deployment` can never again reject a valid 3-day write.
- **Once deployed = frozen (v32, BEFORE any lock/deadline).** A regular sewadar with a `deployments` row (its `department_id` is NOT NULL, so row-exists ⇒ deployed) can no longer be changed or removed by centre users: centre-role UPDATE/DELETE of that `deployments` row AND of its matching `sewadar_consents` row is rejected by `trg_a_freeze_deployed_deploy` / `trg_a_freeze_consent_of_deployed` (function `freeze_deployed_rows()`, `sql/v32_freeze_deployed_sewadars.sql`). Exempt: aso/super_admin; a **normal** Control Panel override (department-scoped for the deployments row, centre-wide-only for the consent row); **undeployed-only overrides never bypass**; VSS exempt. Brand-new deployments (INSERT) stay allowed. The UI mirrors it: these rows show a read-only dept pill + `DEPLOYED` tag, every control is disabled, bulk actions skip them, and persist never writes/deletes them.
- **Parallel sessions no longer clobber each other** (the "my work disappeared with two open tabs" bug). Both editors (Consent & Deploy + VSS) save field-by-field: rows with a saved baseline are PATCHed with ONLY the fields that differ from the last-saved state (`changedConsentFields` in `src/lib/logic.js`), grouped by identical field-set and matched per-row via a quoted PostgREST `or()` filter; brand-new rows are chunked INSERTs. Realtime peer writes coalesce (600 ms debounce), the 1500 ms self-echo right after our own save is skipped (`lastWriteAtRef`), and untouched rows are baseline-synced to the fresh server state on every reload so peer edits stop counting as LOCAL pending edits (queued reloads drain in the persist `finally`).
- **Every deployed sewadar gets a consent row.** The DB raises "No consent recorded for this sewadar" whenever a deployment is written for a sewadar with NO `sewadar_consents` row — an override relaxes only the consent=No check, never a missing row. Since consent rows are only created when consent fields change, an untouched consent=No sewadar has no row; persist therefore guarantees existence (INSERT with `ignoreDuplicates: true`, chunked) for every sewadar it attempts to deploy, before the deployments write. The set of rows that already exist is tracked from the load-time `sewadar_consents` fetch (`consentExistsRef`).

## Database Tables

### `deployment_schedules`
Named visit. `name`, `status` (`open` → `done`), `deadline timestamptz`, `created_by`. Old `consent_locked`/`deployment_locked`/`consent_open`/`deployment_open` are gone (migrated in `v2_deployment_redesign.sql`). Unique index on `lower(name)`.

### `deployment_departments`
Reference list + restriction rules (`min_days int`, `requires_stay_at_bhati bool`, `requires_initiated bool`, `is_active bool`).

### `centre_allocations`
Super-admin allocation: `(schedule, department, centre)` → `max_count`. Only parent centres.

### `sewadar_consents`
Per-sewadar: `(schedule, centre, badge, consent_given, available_days_count, stay_at_bhati, chair_pass)`. Unique `(schedule_id, centre, badge_number)`.

### `deployments`
Assignments: `(schedule, centre, badge)` → `department_id` (the sewadar's **requested** department), `deployed_department_id` (the **final** department set by ASO/super_admin, nullable), `status` (`requested`). Unique `(schedule_id, centre, badge_number)`.

### `prev_year_deployments`
Reference data from the previous visit, matched by `badge_number` (PK): `prev_department`, `attendance_reported`. Imported from Excel (`sql/prev_year_deployments_data.sql`, 2415 rows).

### `audit_log`
Soft-audit for destructive actions: schedule/`centre_allocation`/department deletes + `REMOVE_ALL`. Insert policy is `super_admin` via `get_portal_user_role()` (NOT the raw JWT `role` claim).

## Existing Tables Used

- `sewadars` — centre scope, badge_number, sewadar_name, is_initiated, badge_status (ELDERLY excluded)
- `centres` — `name`, `parent_centre` (empty = parent centre); used to resolve parent/child subtree for shared quotas
- `portal_users` — auth, centre, role

## Key Rules & Helpers

- `get_root_centre(centre)` — walks `parent_centre` chain to find the parent centre (SQL + JS copy in `src/lib/logic.js`).
- `get_my_subtree_centres()` — centre names under the caller's centre (itself + descendants); used by RLS so CENTREs manage their own + SC_SPs.
- `get_remaining_quota(schedule, department)` — remaining quota for caller's root centre counting the whole subtree (security definer).
- Deployment inserts/updates validated by trigger `check_deployment` (schedule open, deadline not passed, consent exists + given, not elderly, eligibility, quota). Multi-row inserts additionally guarded by statement-level `check_deployment_batch` (quota bypass protection).
- Consent/deployment edits blocked by `block_after_deadline` trigger once the schedule deadline has passed, status is `done`, or the relevant master switch (`sewadar_deployment_open` / `vss_deployment_open`) is closed. A NULL deadline does NOT block (deadline optional). Centre roles cannot set `deployments.deployed_department_id` — only super_admin can (aso lost that with v20). A v21 Control Panel override (`is_centre_override_open`) bypasses the lock/switch/deadline for its scope — never `done`.
- **v32 deployed-sewadar freeze** (`sql/v32_freeze_deployed_sewadars.sql`): centre-role UPDATE/DELETE on a deployed regular sewadar's `deployments` or `sewadar_consents` row raises (`freeze_deployed_rows()`, triggers `trg_a_freeze_deployed_deploy`/`trg_a_freeze_consent_of_deployed`). Exemptions: aso/super_admin; a normal `is_centre_override_open(schedule, centre, dept)` — passed the ROW's department for `deployments`, `NULL::uuid` (centre-wide only) for consents; VSS badges. Undeployed-only override rows never bypass. INSERT of a brand-new deployment unaffected.
- **`fetchAllRpc` + `RPC_PAGE_SPECS`** (`src/lib/supabase.js`, release 2026-10-03): PostgREST's `db-max-rows` (1000) truncates SETOF RPC results too, so every per-badge attendance/previsit feed pages through `fetchAllRpc` with `count:'exact'` (retry once, then throw on count mismatch; an unknown spec throws — fail-closed) and KPIs, expected denominators, workbook sheets and previsit totals stay complete. Registered: `attendance_sewadar_summary`, `attendance_day_badges`, `previsit_sewadars`, `previsit_deployed`; `previsit_summary` stays single-shot (server-aggregated). A new RPC returning per-badge rows MUST be added to `RPC_PAGE_SPECS` or its caller fails loudly instead of silently rendering a 1000-row prefix.

## Database Tables (Control Panel, v21)

### `centre_overrides`
Presence of a row = deployment writing OPEN for that scope: `schedule_id`, `centre` (`'*'` = ALL centres, else the root CENTRE — SC_SPs inherit), `department_id` (NULL = all departments), `note`, `created_by`. Unique index on `(schedule_id, centre, COALESCE(department_id, sentinel-uuid))`. Resolved by `is_centre_override_open(schedule, centre, dept)`: a department-scoped row opens only deployments into that department; consent rows reopen only through a centre-wide (NULL dept) override. RLS: read = aso/super_admin; write = super_admin.

### `centre_vss_overrides`
Tri-state VSS knobs per centre: `centre UNIQUE` (`'*'` = all), `creation_open boolean NULL`, `deployment_open boolean NULL` — NULL = inherit the global switch/window. Consumed by `guard_vss_registration_write` (creation gate) and `vss_deploy_open_for_centre` (deployment marking). Client RPC `get_my_effective_gates(schedule)` returns the effective booleans for the caller's centre.

## UI conventions

- The word "Super Admin" never appears in the UI — `ROLE_LABELS.super_admin` displays as **"ASO"** (`src/lib/supabase.js`); all user-facing copy says ASO only.
- **Lock Deployment under-quota acknowledgement** (`ConsentPage.jsx`): when a CENTRE locks while filling any allocated department below its quota, the confirm modal shows a Department / Quota Assigned / Sewadars Allotted table (numbers match the quota bars — effective dept incl. unsaved edits, regular + VSS) and requires a tick-box ("I have checked the list above and confirm that this deployment is correct") before the Lock button enables. Full-quota centres see the plain confirm modal.
- Only a root-CENTRE account sees the Lock Deployment button; SC_SP users see a "Locked at CENTRE level" pill instead (v16 M6 blocks their inserts server-side anyway).
- Incharge picker/staleness logic judges occupancy by the EFFECTIVE department (final else requested — mirrors v17 `trg_check_incharge`), while the lock compulsion (`missingIncharges`) keeps matching persisted requested departments (mirrors v13 `check_centre_lock`).
- **Add-VSS gate** (`VssPage` → `AddVssForm`, v19): centre roles can create VSS registrations only while the ASO's "Add VSS" master switch (VSS Dashboard) is open **and** an open schedule's deadline hasn't passed; the banner states which gate is closed. After the deadline, pending-row Edit/Delete disable too. aso/super_admin bypass both gates (aso is fully read-only since v20). A v21 per-centre tri-state override can force the gate open/closed for one centre. The DB trigger `trg_a_guard_vss_registration` is authoritative. **Since v37 the VSS switch gate binds the `vss_operator` too** (fail-closed for VSS-population rows; regular rows keep the bypass). `VssPage` has ConsentPage-parity deployed freeze (`DEPLOYED` pill, controls disabled, persist/bulk skip), and the `AddVssForm` registration list is paginated via `fetchAllRows` (no 200-row cap).
- **VSFB-assigned = frozen for centres**: once a registration is `assigned` its VSFB number, AddVssForm shows a 🔒 Locked pill and refuses edit/delete (v9 RLS enforces it server-side); roster writes stay super_admin-only (v4). Per-schedule consent/deployment marking is NOT affected.
- **Control Panel override semantics** (v21): opening a centre shows a green "specially opened for your centre" banner on Consent & Deploy / VSS and re-enables editing past a closed switch, passed deadline or centre lock; finalized rows keep their FINAL pills. Per-department unlocks show an amber note that only that department's deployments reopen — consent rows need a centre-wide open. Overrides never change quota bars or eligibility reasons.
